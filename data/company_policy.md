# ShopNest Inc. Customer Support Policy Handbook (v7.3)

## 1. Introduction
ShopNest Inc. is committed to providing outstanding customer experiences. This handbook describes
the policies every support agent, human or AI, must follow. Agents should read this handbook in full
before handling any customer interaction. Policies are reviewed quarterly by the Customer Experience
Council and may be updated at any time.

## 2. Our Values
- Customer obsession: we start with the customer and work backwards.
- Ownership: every agent owns the outcome of the ticket they touch.
- Empathy: acknowledge the customer's feelings before solving the problem.
- Transparency: never hide fees, timelines, or limitations.
- Speed: first response within 2 business hours, resolution within 48 hours.

## 3. Refund Policy
3.1 Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery for any unused item in its
original packaging.
3.2 Items returned between 31 and 60 days are eligible for store credit only.
3.3 Items returned after 60 days are not eligible for refund or credit, except under warranty.
3.4 Digital goods, gift cards, and personalized items are non-refundable.
3.5 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the return
is received at the warehouse.
3.6 Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopNest error.
3.7 Agents may approve refunds up to $150 without supervisor approval. Refunds above $150 require
Tier 2 approval. Refunds above $1,000 require a Finance Ops ticket.
3.8 Partial refunds may be issued for items received with minor cosmetic damage, up to 25% of the
item price, if the customer agrees to keep the item.

## 4. Shipping Policy
4.1 Standard shipping: 5-8 business days. Free on orders over $50.
4.2 Express shipping: 2-3 business days. $12.99 flat fee.
4.3 Overnight shipping: next business day if ordered before 2pm local warehouse time. $29.99.
4.4 International shipping is available to 42 countries. Duties and taxes are the customer's
responsibility unless DDP shipping was selected at checkout.
4.5 If a package is marked delivered but not received, the customer must wait 48 hours before a
lost-package claim can be opened.
4.6 Lost packages are reshipped once at no cost. A second loss on the same order triggers a refund.
4.7 Late deliveries of more than 5 business days beyond the estimate qualify for a shipping fee refund.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Items
5.1 Customers must report damage within 14 days of delivery.
5.2 Photos are required for damage claims over $75.
5.3 Replacements are prioritized over refunds when stock is available.
5.4 Agents should not ask customers to return items worth less than $20; issue a replacement or
refund directly.

## 6. Account and Security
6.1 Never ask a customer for their full password or full card number.
6.2 Identity must be verified with two of: email on file, last 4 of card, billing ZIP, last order ID.
6.3 Password resets are self-service via the "Forgot password" link. Agents may trigger a reset email.
6.4 Accounts locked after 5 failed login attempts unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
6.5 Suspected account takeover must be escalated to Trust & Safety immediately.

## 7. Subscriptions (ShopNest Plus)
7.1 ShopNest Plus costs $9.99/month or $99/year.
7.2 Benefits: free express shipping, 5% back in rewards, early access to sales.
7.3 Monthly plans may be cancelled anytime; access continues until the end of the billing period.
7.4 Annual plans cancelled within 14 days receive a full refund; after 14 days, a prorated refund.
7.5 Agents may offer one free month as a retention gesture once per customer per 12 months.

## 8. Escalation
8.1 Escalate to Tier 2: refunds over $150, repeated contacts (3+) on the same issue, VIP customers.
8.2 Escalate to Legal: any mention of lawsuits, attorneys, regulators, or chargeback disputes over $500.
8.3 Escalate to Trust & Safety: fraud, account takeover, harassment.
8.4 Never promise outcomes that require escalation approval.

## 9. Tone and Communication
9.1 Use the customer's first name.
9.2 Avoid jargon and internal terms such as "WMS", "RMA code", or "Tier 2".
9.3 Keep replies under 150 words unless the issue requires detailed steps.
9.4 Always close with a clear next step and an expected timeline.
9.5 Do not use emojis in formal complaints or legal-adjacent tickets.

## 10. Warranty
10.1 Electronics carry a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty.
10.2 ShopNest Protect extended warranty adds 2 additional years and covers accidental damage.
10.3 Warranty claims require proof of purchase and a description of the defect.

## 11. Gift Orders
11.1 Gift receipts hide prices. Gift recipients may exchange but not refund to the original payment.
11.2 Gift messages are limited to 250 characters.

## 12. Data Privacy
12.1 Customers may request a copy of their data or deletion of their account under applicable law.
12.2 Data requests are routed to privacy@shopnest.example and fulfilled within 30 days.
12.3 Never paste customer PII into external tools.

## 13. Holiday Season Exceptions
13.1 Items purchased between Nov 1 and Dec 24 may be returned until Jan 31.
13.2 Support hours are extended to 7am-11pm local time from Nov 20 through Jan 5.

## 14. Appendix: Glossary
- RMA: Return Merchandise Authorization.
- WMS: Warehouse Management System.
- DDP: Delivered Duty Paid.
- CSAT: Customer Satisfaction Score.
- FCR: First Contact Resolution.
